EVAM Books — browser edition
============================

1. Unzip this folder anywhere on your computer (keep all the files together).
2. Double-click  EVAM-Books.html  — it opens in Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari.
3. You are signed in to an example company. Everything works: documents,
   invoices and payments, bills, expenses, banking, VAT and reports.

Your data is saved in this browser on this computer. Nothing is sent anywhere.
Use the "Browser edition" button (top right) to download a backup, restore one,
reset to the example company, or start with an empty company (setup code: DEMO).

Use one tab at a time. A private/incognito window forgets everything when closed.
