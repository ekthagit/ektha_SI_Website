Reading options

How it works

  • PDFs with selectable text are read directly.
  • Scans, photos and PDFs without usable text are read with text recognition, which can misread characters, so they are always marked for a quick check. The reader downloads once (about 10–20 MB).
  • Files are read on this computer and are not uploaded anywhere.
  • Anything that could not be read with certainty is listed on the document as a check, and the totals always match the original.
  • Imported documents keep their original numbers and are marked Imported until you set their status.
